Hauntscope turns your phone into a paranormal camcorder. Point it at your room, follow the EMF radar and catch the ghosts hiding in your home, right there through the camera in augmented reality.

HOW THE HUNT WORKS
• EMF radar: the closer the ghost, the faster it beeps.
• Ghost Lens: reveal what the naked eye can't see, but watch your battery.
• Capture Beam: hold the ghost inside the ring until it's trapped.
• Spirit Camera: snap evidence photos and share your best shots.

12 GHOSTS, 12 WAYS TO HUNT
From the shy Wisp to the legendary Mara who comes for your light, every ghost has its own body, voice and trick. The Poltergeist teleports, the Banshee shrieks, the Mimic lies to your EMF meter, the Negative exists only on film and the Lurker moves only when you look away.
