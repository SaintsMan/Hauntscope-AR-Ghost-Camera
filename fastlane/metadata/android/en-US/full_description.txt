Hauntscope is a ghost detector game that turns your phone into a paranormal ghost camera. Scan your home with the EMF detector, tune the Spirit Box and catch ghosts right in your room in augmented reality.

Every agent gets a full ghost hunting kit, just in time for Halloween. Follow the signals, find where the ghost hides and trap it before your battery dies.

HOW THE GHOST DETECTOR WORKS
1. Scan your room and let the ghost detector pick up a signal.
2. Follow the EMF radar, the Spirit Box voices and your EVP recordings to close in.
3. Use the thermal camera, night vision or UV flashlight to find its hiding place.
4. Reveal it with the Ghost Lens and trap it with the Capture Beam.
5. Snap a photo for its case file, like a real ghost hunter.

GHOST HUNTING TOOLS
• EMF detector & ghost radar: the closer the ghost, the faster the meter beeps.
• Spirit Box: sweep the radio static until a voice tells you where the ghost is: left, behind you, close.
• EVP recorder: record the silence of the room, play it back and hear what the ghost whispered.
• Thermal camera: ghosts show up as cold spots on the heat map.
• Night vision: switch to the green camcorder view and spot faint shapes in the dark.
• UV flashlight: reveal glowing traces that lead to the ghost's hiding place.
• Ghost Lens: see the ghost with your own eyes, if your battery lasts.
• Capture Beam: hold the ghost in the ring until the trap closes.

A GHOST CAMERA FOR EVIDENCE AND PRANKS
• Spirit camera: snap evidence photos of every ghost and share your best shots.
• Ghost prank photos: place a ghost anywhere in your room, take the picture and scare your friends.
